Community Chat

A place for conversation and posts, designed around respectful participation, moderation, and care for the rights and safety of others.

Write a post

Prototype only: chat is not connected to a publishing service. Submitted text is not sent or published. A production service must implement applicable-law and terms review, copyright reporting, abuse response, privacy controls, and human escalation. No automated filter can guarantee compliance with every jurisdiction or detect every issue.

Moderation and publishing services are not configured.

Moderation workflow blueprint

  1. Validate and rate-limit submissions on the server; keep untrusted content as text.
  2. Apply documented safety, terms-of-service, and copyright review with human escalation and an appeal process.
  3. Hold uncertain or reported posts from public display until review is complete.
  4. Provide reporting, privacy controls, and a transparent response process.

These are product requirements, not a statement that this prototype provides legal compliance or legal advice. Rules and obligations vary by location and context.
