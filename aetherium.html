The Logos Hall

Balance insight with compassion

Within this spiritual framework, Adam can represent awakening reason and Eve emotion or feeling. Their dialogue invites a whole-minded response: neither discernment nor empathy should be abandoned.

Good and evil are considered through the consequences of choices, with humility about human fallibility. The cry of Abel's spirit becomes a call to notice harm, seek justice, and repair what can be repaired. Christed principles here mean compassion, truthfulness, and responsibility in practice—not a claim of infallibility.

The Cain and Abel story can serve as a warning against allowing righteous intent to become resentment or harm. Pause, listen, seek counsel, and correct mistakes before good work is made an excuse for them.
